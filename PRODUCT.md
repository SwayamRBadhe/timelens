# TimeLens
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Next.js App Router, strict TypeScript, React, Tailwind, PostgreSQL, Drizzle, Zod, date-fns, Lucide. Vercel hosting and Neon-compatible PostgreSQL.
## Product Purpose
See the world on any day in history. Explore a cross-section of sourced events by date, region, country, and category. This is an information product, not a simulation.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Gregorian dates from 1500. Shareable date pages, stored-event search, previous/next day, transparent source attribution. No authentication, payments, or separate backend. Seed records keep demonstrations usable without an external provider.
## Brand Commitments
Sophisticated history and modern editorial design. Typography and spacing lead. Avoid corporate dashboards, neon, excessive gradients and rounded cards.
## Evidence on Hand
Curated source-linked historical seed records. Coverage is partial and must never imply a complete account of the world.
